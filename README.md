## 🏛️ Grounded Factual Standards
Every video reel published here adheres to strict journalistic criteria:
* **Zero-Hallucination Claims**: Every statistic, vote outcome, and quote is verified directly against official London borough agendas and public committee minutes.
* **Authentic 35mm Civic Imagery**: Zero synthetic faces, deepfakes, or politicians. All visual media is framed purely on municipal architecture, highways, and public realm environments.
* **Frame-Accurate Delivery**: Composed in 1080×1920 vertical 9:16 at 30fps with British broadcast narration and official public record audit citations.
