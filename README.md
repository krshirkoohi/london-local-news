This is an **independently developed demonstration project by Kavia Shirkoohi**, exploring short-form video reporting based on publicly available London council and local-news material. It was created to demonstrate a possible approach to [London Local](https://londonlocal.news/) news videos. **It is not an official London Local or Open Council Network project, and the videos have not been approved or published by their editorial team.**

Visit the interactive web player: **[krshirkoohi.github.io/london-local-news](https://krshirkoohi.github.io/london-local-news/)**

## Approach and limitations

- **Source-based reporting:** The demos aim to trace material claims to council records, consultations, and other linked source documents. Source references are provided for inspection, but this prototype is **not a substitute for independent fact-checking**.
- **Programmatic video:** The showcase presents vertical 9:16 video reports with British-English narration, graphics, and illustrative footage.
- **Editorial controls:** The intended workflow includes human review before use by a news organisation. This public demo is **not** an editor-approved production newsroom, a live publishing integration, or a validated automated editorial review system.
- **Illustrative media:** AI-generated scenes are intended to provide atmosphere, not to depict actual recorded events. Any factual or visual errors should be corrected before editorial use.
- **Development status:** Further work on editorial review tools, evaluation, audience analytics, and cost measurement is planned rather than represented as completed functionality.

These are experimental demonstrations. No claim of zero factual errors, guaranteed performance, or institutional endorsement is made.
